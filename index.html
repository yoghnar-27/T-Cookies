Our Story

Unhurried, by design

Coffee and Cookies has quietly become the neighbourhood's go-to for a cup that's actually good and a table nobody rushes you off. No loud playlists, no clatter — just warm ambient light, comfortable seating and a menu built for lingering.

Guests keep coming back for the same reasons: a kitchen that treats comfort food seriously, a room that photographs as good as it feels, and a team that remembers your order by the third visit.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about visiting Coffee and Crisps Cafe in Saroornagar.

What are the daily operating hours of the cafe?
We are open every single day from 11:00 AM to 10:45 PM, including weekends and public holidays.
Is valet parking available at Coffee and Crisps?
Yes! We offer complimentary valet parking at our entrance so you can arrive stress-free without looking for parking along Road Number 1.
Do you have both vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes?
Yes, our kitchen serves a broad variety of vegetarian and non-vegetarian comfort food — from loaded cheese fries, peri-peri chicken crumbs, and wood-fired pizzas to artisanal coffees, fruit coolers, and rich desserts.
Do I need a reservation, or can I walk in?
Walk-ins are always welcome! However, for weekend evenings or larger group gatherings, reserving ahead is recommended. You can call us directly at +91 77998 44644 to hold a table.
Is there outdoor seating available?
Yes, we offer both a cozy indoor air-conditioned space and a relaxed, garden-lit outdoor seating area perfect for cool evening conversations.
What are the most recommended menu items by regulars?
Guests consistently love our Signature Cappuccino, Loaded Cheese Fries, Peri-Peri Chicken Crumbs, Sizzling Chocolate Brownie, and Brownie Hot Chocolate.
Where is the cafe located exactly?
We are located at Road Number 1, Doctors Colony, Saroornagar, Hyderabad, Telangana 500035. Tap 'Get Directions' in the Visit section for exact turn-by-turn map navigation.
