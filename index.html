Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about visiting T & Cookies Cafe in Green Park.

What are the daily operating hours of the cafe?
We are open every single day from 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM, including weekends and public holidays.
Is valet parking available at T & Cookies?
Yes! We offer complimentary valet parking at our entrance so you can arrive stress-free without searching for parking on Lotus Avenue.
