Our Story

Unhurried, by design

Tucked into Road Number 1 in Saroornagar, Coffee and Crisps has quietly become the neighbourhood's go-to for a cup that's actually good and a table nobody rushes you off. No loud playlists, no clatter — just warm ambient light, comfortable seating and a menu built for lingering.

Guests keep coming back for the same reasons: a kitchen that treats comfort food seriously, a room that photographs as good as it feels, and a team that remembers your order by the third visit.

  • Valet parking & spotless washrooms
  • Calm, unhurried evenings — even on weekends
  • Generous portions, attentive service
